PART 1

📚 Lexical Retrieval: Semantic-Field Cycling

Click "Sprint" and read each target sentence aloud before the countdown ends to build instant academic vocabulary access.

🏷️ Hypothesis /haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/ (n.)

A proposed explanation made on the basis of limited evidence as a starting point for further investigation.

Example: "While I understand your point, I think our hypothesis needs more testing."
🏷️️ Sustainable /səˈsteɪnəbl/ (adj.)

Able to be maintained at a certain rate or level without depleting natural resources.

Example: "Why don't we consider a more sustainable energy source for the model?"
🏷️ Efficiency /ɪˈfɪʃnsi/ (n.)

The state or quality of being efficient; achieving maximum productivity with minimum wasted effort.

Example: "Could we look at another option to improve the turbine's energy efficiency?"
🏷️ Prototype /ˈprəʊtətaɪp/ (n.)

A first or preliminary model of a machine or device from which other forms are developed.

Example: "While I like this design, building a working prototype will take two weeks."
🏷️ Alternative /ɔːlˈtɜːrnətɪv/ (n./adj.)

Available as another possibility or choice.

Example: "Why don't we consider solar power as an alternative to biomass?"
🏷️ Renewable /rɪˈnuːəbl/ (adj.)

Naturally replenished energy sources such as wind, solar, and water.

Example: "Could we look at another renewable energy model for our presentation?"

Polite Disagreement Softener 01

Express polite disagreement regarding project speed:

understand / point / think / too slow
"While I understand your point, I think this method is too slow."
Suggestion Formulation 02

Propose an idea using the question form:

why don't / consider / sustainable energy
"Why don't we consider a sustainable energy design?"
Alternative Inquiry 03

Ask to explore another option for efficiency:

could / look at / another option / efficiency
"Could we look at another option to improve our efficiency?"
Hypothesis Defense 04

Defend your scientific hypothesis politely:

while / understand / think / hypothesis / correct
"While I understand your point, I think our hypothesis is correct."
Prototype Suggestion 05

Suggest building a working prototype:

why don't / build / working prototype / fair
"Why don't we build a working prototype for the fair?"
Alternative Proposal 06

Propose an alternative renewable material:

could / look at / alternative / solar power
"Could we look at solar power as an alternative energy source?"

Academic Discourse Watchlist:
