🎮 Level-Up: Vocabulary Frames

Review the terms and complete the sentence frames below.

Resume (CV)
A document summarizing your work experience, education, and professional skills.
Example: She submitted her updated ________ to the hiring manager before the interview.
Work Experience
Knowledge or practical skills gained while working in a specific job.
Example: I have gained valuable ________ in retail and customer support over the past two years.
Strengths
The positive qualities, skills, or abilities you are particularly good at.
Example: One of my greatest ________ is my ability to communicate clearly under pressure.
Qualification
An official record or skill that makes you suitable for a specific position.
Example: Having a customer service certification is an excellent ________ for this position.

🔍 Quick Drill: Fix the Mistakes

Click on the sentences below to correct common grammar and vocabulary mistakes. One sentence is already correct!

?
I am working as a sales assistant for three years.
I have worked as a sales assistant for three years. (Or: I have been working as a sales assistant for three years.)
Present Perfect rule: Use "have worked" or "have been working" to describe an experience that started in the past and continues into the present.
?
I am very reliable and work well under pressure.
Correct!
This sentence correctly uses the present simple to describe ongoing personal qualities and workplace strengths.
?
Could you tell me what is the working schedule?
Could you tell me what the working schedule is?
Indirect question rule: In polite embedded questions ("Could you tell me..."), use normal statement word order (Subject + Verb), not question order.
?
If I will get the job, I will start next week.
If I get the job, I will start next week.
First conditional rule: Use the Present Simple ("get") in the if-clause, and "will + verb" in the main result clause.
